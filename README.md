# MegaCDExtender
A solution to allow for easier testing of Sega/Mega CD units

Ever had to troubleshoot a Mega CD? Everyone knows what a chore it can be to have to have the Genesis/MD sitting precariously above, making it difficult to probe the board. 

This is a simple extension board to allow for running the Genesis/MD off to the side, leaving the board open for analysis!

*** USE THIS AT YOUR OWN RISK. I HAVE TESTED THIS AND IT WORKS PERFECTLY, BUT I AM NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR IMPROPER ASSEMBLY CAUSING DAMAGE ***

## Materials Needed
1 MD Side PCB

1 CD Side PCB

![](/_images/bare_pcb.jpeg)

4 60 (2x30) pin IDC connectors (https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256808989392536.html)

1 60 pin RIGHT ANGLE Card Edge Connector (notches should be oriented like the photo) (https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256803460752665.html)

![](/_images/Connectors.jpeg)

2 60 pin IDC cables (https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256809525176986.html)

![](/_images/IDC_Cables.jpeg)

## Assembly Instructions

### 1. Assemble the IDC and Card Edge connectors
Make sure the notches on the IDC connectors align with the indicated notch on the silkscreen.
![](/_images/Assembled_PCB.jpeg)

### 2. Insert The First IDC cable as shown
![](/_images/First_cable.jpeg)

### 3. Insert the Second IDC cable
This completes the assembly. It is recommended to verify pin to pin connectivity from the 60 pin slot on the MD side to the 60 pin edge on the CD side.
![](/_images/Complete.jpeg)
